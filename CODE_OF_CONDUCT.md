# Code of Conduct

DiskPie welcomes people of every background and experience level. We want
project spaces to be useful, patient, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while still discussing evidence plainly.
- Give feedback about work and ideas, never about a person's worth or identity.
- Make room for questions, accessibility needs, and different communication
  styles.
- Credit contributions and respect licenses, privacy, and clean-room boundaries.
- Accept correction, repair harm where possible, and move the work forward.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, deliberate safety violations, and publishing another person's
private information are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing
private or sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily limit participation,
or permanently ban participation when needed to protect the community. Reports
will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant in an investigation is itself a violation.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is
representing DiskPie.
